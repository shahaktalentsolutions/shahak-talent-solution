Privacy Policy

Shahak Talent Solution respects the privacy of people who use this website. This policy explains, in general terms, what information may be collected through candidate registrations and employer enquiries and how it may be used.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the form you use, information may include your name, contact details, location, professional qualifications, experience, employment information, skills, preferred roles, LinkedIn profile, CV/resume, company details and recruitment requirements.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to review recruitment profiles, communicate about suitable opportunities, understand employer hiring requirements, coordinate recruitment discussions, respond to enquiries and maintain recruitment records.

3. Resume processing

Candidate resumes may be stored in a private recruitment storage location and accessed by authorised personnel for recruitment-related purposes. A resume may be shared with a prospective employer only when relevant to a recruitment process and subject to the applicable recruitment engagement and candidate communication.

4. Storage and security

We aim to use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for information collected through the website. Access to internal recruitment records should be limited to authorised users. No internet-based storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data retention

Recruitment information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment operations, legal or business recordkeeping, or to consider a profile for future suitable opportunities, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Your choices

You may contact Shahak Talent Solution to ask about information associated with your submission or to request an update or removal where appropriate and technically/legally possible. Requests may require verification of identity.

7. Third-party services

The website may use services such as Google Sheets, Google Drive, Google Apps Script, website hosting and WhatsApp Business Platform to process submissions or communications when these services are configured by the website administrator. Their own terms and privacy practices may also apply.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact business@shahaktalent.com or shahaktalentsolutions@gmail.com.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated as the website, recruitment processes or applicable requirements change. The current version published on this website will apply to future use.
